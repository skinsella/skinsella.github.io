I am Full Professor of Economics at the University of Limerick and Economic Advisor to An Tánaiste and the Minister for Finance.

These days I am interested in macroeconomics, public policy, and innovation. 

My research is archived at [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7943-4797) and citations, etc., at [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Duqr7rIAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao). 

My academic CV is available [here as PDF](https://stephenkinsella.net/cv/cv.pdf). You can sometimes, although rarely, find me on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/stephenkinsella.bsky.social). 

I write a weekly blog at my [Substack](https://stephenkinsella.substack.com).
