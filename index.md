I am Economic Advisor to An Tánaiste and the Minister for Finance Simon Harris.

My research is archived at [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7943-4797) and citations, etc., at [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Duqr7rIAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao). 

You can sometimes, although rarely, find me on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/stephenkinsella.bsky.social). 

I write a weekly blog at my [Substack](https://stephenkinsella.substack.com). 

I'm often asked to speak at events, for boards, and for other things like that. If you're interested in that, talk to the lovely people at [Personally Speaking]([url](https://personallyspeaking.com/speaker/prof-stephen-kinsella/)).
