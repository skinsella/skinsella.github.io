# Second error search and model decisions

The pension top-up reduction is applied to both the pension envelope and opening deficit, preserving the retained-pension residual. It follows the supplied Doyle assumption and remains a sensitivity rather than a cohort-based valuation.

The State debt check now uses Republic GNI* plus NI output after unity. This is an approximate mixed income measure, while the annual Republic burden still uses Republic GNI* alone and the Republic-only debt ratio remains available separately.

Conflicting non-null custom harmonisation inputs are rejected under a named case. Users must choose custom or supply the named preset values.

The pension adjustment is exposed with its source on the specialist page. The old reference builder delegates to the component reference instead of failing against current variables.

The 2024 GNI* outturn and EU contribution line remain pending decisions and are not activated. The frozen tax adjustment is also retained, with its source-unit and policy-regime limitations still requiring reconciliation.

All current tests pass, including four-case paper reproduction, pension reconciliation and conflicting-setting rejection. No central harmonisation case is selected.
