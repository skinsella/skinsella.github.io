The status quo is not costless. This page separates the Irish Exchequer incidence of unity from the Northern resource deficit currently financed inside the UK fiscal system.

Do not subtract these series without qualification. The unity series is a conditional Irish Exchequer transition cost. The status-quo series is a Northern expenditure-revenue gap financed by the UK.
