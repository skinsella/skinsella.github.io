What a defensible full module needs

  • Administrative tax and benefit microdata or a validated microsimulation model
  • Household composition, housing tenure, disability and childcare costs
  • Indirect tax incidence and regional price differences
  • Valuation of service changes separately from cash income
  • Labour supply and take-up responses
