Assessment scope

Household earned income remains unassessed. Output, fiscal transfers and the assumed growth gap are separate accounts, not evidence of an income gain.

The research data separate capital, trade and net FDI output terms from assumed growth. These terms are mechanical experiments until calibrated, and cannot supply an economic pass.

Evidence needed before scenarios A and B can run

Net EU support, matched FDI intensities and receipts, integration inventories and gravity-to-output estimates remain missing. A convergence-gated pay timetable also needs an explicit rule.

No favourable central values have been invented. Named scenario exports require a committed settings file, an adverse twin, the same UK path and an executable fingerprint.
